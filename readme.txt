FGC Matchup Graphics by TAIT-SAMA

Makes PNG images. By default the background is **transparent**, so you can put the PNG on top of a moving video background in your editor or in OBS.

Settings (left side, all tabs)

- **Theme:** Jamaican (black, green, gold), MK (red and black), or Basic (blue and slate). It changes both the app and your exported images.
- **Title, subtitle, footer:** the text on every card. Put your Twitch or YouTube link in the footer.
- **Background:** *Transparent* for video overlays, *Themed pattern* for a standalone image, or *Solid dark*.

Tab: Matchup cards

1. **Faces (optional):** click a name to add that player's character image. Portrait or square images work best, with the face in the upper half.
2. **Matchups:** add them one at a time, paste lines like `Kemar vs Shanice`, or paste a list of players and click *Add all pairings* to make every combination.
3. **Layout:**
   - *Single matchup* is one full-screen card per matchup. You can add a round or note to each.
   - *Schedule* puts up to 5 matchups on one image and splits longer lists across several images automatically.
   - *Banner strip* is a slim 1920×300 bar for the lower part of a video.
4. Click a matchup to preview it, then **Download this PNG** or **Download all PNGs**.

Tab: Big card

A large Player A vs Player B card for intros or on-screen display. Type two names and a tagline such as "First to 3". *Use selected matchup* fills the names from your list, and *Swap sides* flips them.

Tab: Winner card

A large winner graphic with the player's name, an optional score, the opponent and an extra line such as the round. *Player A won* and *Player B won* fill it in from the selected matchup.

Using the PNGs in a video

Drop the PNG on a layer **above** your video or background in your editor. In OBS, add it as an *Image* source. The transparent areas let the video show through.

Good to know

- The grey checkerboard in the preview only shows where the image is transparent. It isn't part of the PNG.
- The banner layout has no footer, because it's too tight.
- In Schedule layout, per-matchup notes aren't used. Use the default subtitle for the round instead.
- Face images, matchups and settings are saved in this browser only, and the two apps don't share them. Add faces in each app separately.

## Troubleshooting

**My image won't upload or "storage is full".** Use a smaller image. Images are stored in the browser, which has a limited amount of space.

**Headings look plain.** The bold condensed headings use the Impact font, which is on most Windows and Mac computers. Others get a similar fallback.

## A note on images

These tools don't include any game characters or logos. Add your own images, such as screenshots or artwork you're allowed to use.
